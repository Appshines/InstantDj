InstantDj yakozwe n’umwe mu ba-DJ b’inzobere mu mbyino za Ballroom, akaba n’umubyinnyi n’umuteza porogaramu, agamije gukora porogaramu ya DJ yoroshye gukoresha — idafite uburemere n’ikiguzi bya porogaramu za DJ z’umwuga, ariko ifite imikorere myinshi cyane kurusha porogaramu isanzwe icuranga umuziki cyangwa itambutsa amajwi.  
Imbere muri porogaramu harimo uburyo bwo gutunganya amajwi bw’umwuga burimo kugenzura imiterere y’ijwi, guhindura umuvuduko, kompreseri, limiteri, kumenya agace katagira ijwi ku ntangiriro no ku musozo, hamwe n’ingaruka nyinshi — bigatanga ijwi rikomeye kandi riringaniye, rikumvikana kimwe neza kuva ku ndirimbo imwe kugera ku yindi.  
Ushobora gutegura indirimbo ikurikira uko ushaka ukoresheje na terefone, mu gihe abitabiriye ibirori bumva indirimbo iri gucuranga, cyangwa ukareka umugoroba wose ugakomeza wikorera, ufite inzibacyuho zoroshye ariko zisobanutse neza.

Ibice by’ingenzi

🎧 Kumviriza mbere mu buryo bubangikanye nk’uko DJ nyawe abikora  
InstantDj ishyigikira ibisohoka by’amajwi bibiri bitandukanye rwose cyangwa ibikoresho bibiri bitandukanye. Ku gisohoka nyamukuru ni ho umuziki w’abitabiriye ibirori ucikira, naho ku gisohoka cya terefone ni ho wumvira indirimbo ikurikira mu ibanga, ugashaka aho yatangirira neza kandi ugategura inzibacyuho. Ubu buryo bw’imikorere ni bwo butandukanya porogaramu icuranga umuziki gusa no kuba DJ nyakuri.

🎚️ Byikora byose cyangwa ukoresheje intoki  
Iyo ukoresheje Gucuranga byikora, urutonde rw’indirimbo yawe rukomeza gucuranga rwonyine kandi buri ndirimbo ikinjira buhoro mu ikurikira — bikaba byiza ku birori ushaka kwitaho ibindi. Igihe cyose ubishakiye, ushobora kongera gufata igenzura: igihe cyo kuzamura cyangwa kugabanya ijwi, igipimo indirimbo zibarizwamo, ndetse no gutangiza indirimbo ikurikira mbere, byose ushobora kubishyiraho uko ushaka kandi ukabibika bitandukanye kuri buri ndirimbo.

🎛️ Ijwi ry’umwuga rifite ubujyakuzimu  
Inyuma y’imigaragarire isobanutse hari uburyo bukomeye bwo gutunganya amajwi: igenzura risanzwe ry’amatsinda atatu y’ijwi (amajwi aremereye, ayo hagati n’ayoroheje), igenzura ritandukanye ry’umuvuduko n’ubuhagarike bw’ijwi, kompreseri, limiteri/Maximizeri, Harmonic Exciteri, kwagura ijwi rya stereo, Transientformeri, echo ifite igenamiterere ryatoranyijwe ryinshi, ndetse na chorus, flanger na phaser. Normalizeri ihindagurika ihita inganiza ibice by’indirimbo byafashwe ku nzego zitandukanye z’ijwi, ku buryo nta na kimwe cyongera kwigaragaza cyane cyangwa ngo kibure kumvikana. Igenamiterere ryose rishobora kubikwa burundu kuri buri ndirimbo.

🥁 BPM ihore igaragara  
Kumenya BPM byikora bigena umuvuduko w’indirimbo zawe — ku buryo buhamye ku ndirimbo yose, ndetse no mu buryo buhinduka ako kanya hafi y’aho indirimbo igeze gucuranga. Ku ndirimbo zifite ubwoko cyangwa umwimerere w’imbyino, iyo hashyizweho imipaka ya BPM, hagaragara ibice by’amabara by’umuvuduko hamwe n’ishyirwa mu rwego ryumvikana kuva kuri “gahoro cyane” kugeza kuri “vuba cyane”. Ibi bigufasha guhuza umuvuduko wo gucuranga n’ubwoko bw’imbyino bujyana n’indirimbo, ku buryo bunoze ku bantu bari kubyina.

📂 Umuziki wawe utunganijwe neza  
Mucukumbuzi y’indirimbo isaka mu bubiko bw’amadosiye no mu mafolderi yawe mu kanya gato — ushobora gukoresha uburyo bwa Explorer cyangwa ugahitamo amafolderi yawe bwite, ndetse ukabishakisha no mu mafolderi ayarimo. Tondeka hakurikijwe izina, itariki yahinduriweho, igihe yaheruka gucurangwa, BPM, uburebure cyangwa amanota. Amadosiye mashya ahita agaragara kubera gukurikirana amafolderi, kandi ushobora gukurura indirimbo ukoresheje Drag & Drop ukazishyira mu bubiko bwawe cyangwa ku rutonde rw’indirimbo. Ukoresheje amanota y’inyenyeri hamwe n’amoko y’umuziki n’ibyiciro by’amabara, ushobora gucunga n’ububiko bunini bw’umuziki bitakugoye. Ibiranga birambuye dosiye y’amajwi bikwereka imiterere, bitrate, urwego rw’ijwi, ihindagurika ryaryo, uko imirongo y’amajwi ikurikirana n’amateka yose yo gucuranga.

💾 Urutonde rw’indirimbo rugendana nawe  
Bika urutonde rw’indirimbo rwose ushaka rufite amazina, hanyuma urufungure ukanda rimwe. Urutonde rwawe ruhita rubikwa ku bubiko bw’amadosiye bumwe n’ubwo umuziki ukomokamo — bityo ushobora kurugeraho aho ari ho hose ukoresheje USB cyangwa disiki yo hanze, utitaye kuri mudasobwa urwo rutonde rwakoreweho. Urutonde ruri gukoreshwa rushobora guhita rubikwa igihe usohotse muri porogaramu, hanyuma rukagarurwa ubutaha uyitangije.

📱 Telefoni yawe iba igenzura rya kure n’igikoresho gisohora amajwi atambuka  
Koresha igenzura rya kure ryinjijwe muri porogaramu (nta yindi porogaramu isabwa gushyirwaho) ukoresheje igikoresho icyo ari cyo cyose kigendanwa. Sikana kode ya QR maze ugenzure InstantDj aho waba uri hose: tangiza cyangwa uhagarike gucuranga, hindura indirimbo, genzura ijwi, hindura uko indirimbo zikurikirana ku rutonde, kandi ukoreshe mucukumbuzi y’indirimbo yo kuri telefoni wongereho izindi ndirimbo. Niba ubishaka, ushobora no kohereza amajwi ari gucuranga ako kanya kuri telefoni yawe, igihe usohotse ahabera ibirori ariko ukaba ushaka ko ibirori bikomeza.

⌚ Kubara neza mbere igihe indirimbo zizatandariraho  
Igihe buri ndirimbo itegereje ku rutonde izatangirira gihita kibarwa kandi kikerekanwa. Bityo uhora uzi igihe indirimbo izatangirira, ukabasha gutegura neza gahunda y’igihe y’ibirori. Kubara igihe bigera ku isegonda, kandi bikita ku bihe washyizeho byo kuzamura no kugabanya ijwi, ibihe byo gutegereza, ihinduka ry’umuvuduko n’ibindi.

🔌 Yizewe kuri buri gitaramo  
Igisohoka nyamukuru n’igisohoka cya terefone bishobora gushyirwa ku bikoresho bihoraho bitandukanye, bikahabwa amazina yihariye kandi bigahita byongera gukoreshwa igihe igikoresho kibonetse. Iyo igikoresho cy’amajwi gikuweho ku bw’impanuka, InstantDj ikomeza kugishakisha inyuma, ikongera igatangiza gucuranga iyo cyagarutse — cyangwa igahindukira ku kindi gikoresho wahisemo. Byongeye kandi, porogaramu igenzura ibikoresho by’amajwi ikoresheje porogaramu za sisitemu zitandukanyijwe, ku buryo amajwi asohoka neza kandi nta guhagarara n’iyo sisitemu yaba iri gukora cyane — kugira ngo amajwi atazimira igihe ari ngombwa.

⌨️ Byihuse ukoresheje klaviye  
Ibikorwa bikunze gukoreshwa bishobora gushyirwa ku rufunguzo rwihariye wahitamo — kuva ku kumviriza mbere no gusimbuka mu gucuranga ukoresheje terefone, kugeza ku gushyira indirimbo watoranyije ku rutonde. Gutegura umuziki bigenda neza kandi byoroshye, ndetse n’iyo ukoresha akabaho k’imfunguzo gashobora gutegurwa.

💡 Itunganijwe kandi yiteguye gutangira ako kanya  
Imigaragarire igezweho y’amabara yijimye iteguye neza, yumvikana mu buryo bworoshye kandi yagenewe gukora nta nkomyi. Uburyo bw’ikirahure cyuzuye bushyira umuziki ku isonga, nta birangaza.

🔒 Byose bikorerwa kuri telefoni cyangwa mudasobwa yawe gusa  
InstantDj ikorana gusa n’amadosiye yawe ari ku gikoresho cyawe. Nta kohereza amadosiye, nta kwiyandikisha, nta gukurikirana ibikorwa, nta konti — kandi nta murandasi usabwa. Icyitonderwa: Kugira ngo ubone uburyo bwo gukoresha telefoni, birumvikana ko ukeneye umuyoboro w’imbere waho kandi/cyangwa umurongo wa murandasi, bitewe n’uko wateguye uburyo bwo gukoresha telefoni.

🔇 Si iyo gukorera DJ umwuga  
Iyi porogaramu ntiyagenewe kuvanga umuziki nk’umwuga ukoresheje decks nyinshi, sampleri, loops, MIDI controllers, ibikoresho byo hanze, kuvanga umuziki ako kanya, scratch, guhuza imirindiro, guhuza tonalité n’ibindi.
