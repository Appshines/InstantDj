I-InstantDj yaphuhliswa ngu-DJ weBallroom, umdanisi kunye nomphuhlisi wesoftware ozinikeleyo, ngenjongo yokudala usetyenziso lwe-DJ olulula ukulusebenzisa - ngaphandle kobunzima neendleko zesoftware yobuchwephesha ye-DJ, kodwa luneempawu ezininzi ngakumbi kunesidlali somculo okanye sokusasaza esilula.
Ngaphakathi kolu setyenziso kusetyenziswa inkqubo yobuchwephesha yokulungisa isandi enolawulo lwesandi, uhlengahlengiso lwesantya, icompressor, ilimiter, ukufunyanwa kokuthula ekuqaleni nasekupheleni kunye neziphumo ezininzi - ukuze kubekho isandi esinamandla nesilinganayo, esivakala kakuhle ngokulinganayo ukusuka kwingoma ukuya kwenye.
Lungisa ingoma elandelayo ngokuzolileyo usebenzisa ii-headphone, ngelixa abaphulaphuli sele bemamele ingoma edlalayo, okanye uvumele iingokuhlwa zonke ziqhube ngokuzenzekelayo ngotshintsho olugudileyo kodwa olucacileyo phakathi kweengoma.

Iindawo ekugxilwe kuzo

🎧 Mamela kwangaphambili ngaxeshanye njengaye u-DJ wokwenene
I-InstantDj ixhasa iziphumo ezimbini zesandi ezahluke ngokupheleleyo okanye izixhobo ezibini. Kwisiphumo esiphambili umculo udlalela abaphulaphuli bakho, ngelixa kwisiphumo see-headphone umamela ingoma elandelayo ngaxeshanye, ungaviwa ngabanye, ukhangela indawo efanelekileyo yokuqala kwaye ulungiselele utshintsho. Yiyo kanye le ndlela yokusebenza eyahlula isidlali esilula kumsebenzi wokwenene we-DJ.

🎚️ Ngokuzenzekelayo ngokupheleleyo okanye ngesandla
Ngokudlala ngokuzenzekelayo, i-playlist yakho idlala ngokwayo kwaye ingoma nganye idibane ngobunono nale ilandelayo - ilungele amatheko apho ufuna ukujongana nezinye izinto. Nanini na xa ufuna, ungaphinda uzithathele ulawulo: amaxesha okuvela nokuphela kwesandi, ukudibana kweengoma kunye nokuqala kwangaphambili kwengoma elandelayo zinokusetwa ngokukhululekileyo, zize zigcinwe ngokwahlukeneyo kwingoma nganye.

🎛️ Isandi sobuchwephesha esinobunzulu
Emva komphezulu ocacileyo kukho inkqubo enamandla yokulungisa isandi: ulawulo lwesandi lwamaqela amathathu oluqhelekileyo (i-bass, i-mid kunye ne-treble), ulawulo olwahlukileyo lwesantya noluphakamo lwethoni, i-echo, isandi sendawo, icompressor, ilimiter/maximizer, i-Harmonic Exciter, ukwandiswa kwe-stereo, i-Transient Shaper enee-presets ezininzi kunye ne-chorus, i-flanger ne-phaser. I-normalizer eguqukayo ilinganisa ngokuzenzekelayo iindawo zengoma ezirekhodwe ngeevolumu ezahlukeneyo, ukuze kungabikho nto igqama kakhulu okanye ithi shwaka. Zonke iisetingi zinokugcinwa ngokusisigxina kwingoma nganye.

✀ Ukwahlulwa komkhondo wesandi nge-AI ukuya kuthi ga kwiitraki ezi-6
Emva kokukhutshelwa kwemodeli ye-AI efanelekileyo, ukuya kutsho kwiitraki zesandi ezi-6 zinokwahlulwa ngokuzenzekelayo: amazwi, amagubu, i-bass kunye nezinye izixhobo zomculo. Ngale ndlela unokugxininisa okanye uthulise ngamandla iitraki ezizimeleyo. KwiWindows neLinux, ukwahlulwa komkhondo wesandi nge-AI kuxhaswa yi-NVIDIA CUDA, nto leyo eyenza ukusetyenzwa kube malunga namaxesha alishumi ngokukhawuleza kune-CPU.

🥁 I-BPM ihlala ibonakala
Ukufunyanwa kwe-BPM okuzenzekelayo kumisela isantya seengoma zakho - ngokusisigxina kuyo yonke ingoma nangokuguqukayo ngexesha lokwenyani kufutshane nendawo yokudlala yangoku. Kwiingoma ezinohlobo okanye uhlobo oluthile, xa kukho iindawo ze-BPM ezichaziweyo, kuboniswa iindawo zesantya ezinemibala kunye nokuhlelwa okuqondakalayo ukusuka "kucotha kakhulu" ukuya "kukhawuleza kakhulu". Ngale ndlela unokuhlengahlengisa isantya sokudlala ngokuhambelana nohlobo lomdaniso wengoma kunye nabadanisi bakho.

📂 Umculo wakho, uhlelwe ngokugqibeleleyo
Isikhangeli seengoma sikhangela iidrive neefolda zakho ngokukhawuleza okukhulu - nokuba usebenzisa indlela ye-Explorer okanye iifolda zakho ozikhethayo ngokukhululekileyo, xa ufuna njalo siquke neefolda ezingaphantsi ngokuphindaphindiweyo. Hlela ngegama, ngomhla wohlengahlengiso, ngexesha lokugqibela lokudlala, nge-BPM, ngobude okanye nge-reyithingi. Iifayile ezintsha zivela ngokuzenzekelayo ngenxa yokubekwa esweni kweefolda, kwaye nge-Drag & Drop unokutsalela iingoma ngqo kwingqokelela okanye kwi-playlist yakho. Ngeereyithingi zeenkwenkwezi kunye neentlobo neendidi ezinemibala, ugcina nolona luhlu lukhulu lomculo luphantsi kolawulo ngaphandle kobunzima. Iinkcukacha ezineenkcukacha zefayile yesandi zibonisa ifomathi, i-bitrate, inqanaba, i-dynamics, impendulo yefrikwensi kunye nembali epheleleyo yokudlala.

💾 Iiplaylist ezihamba nawe
Gcina iiplaylist ezininzi ezibizwa ngamagama kwaye uzilayishe ngokucofa kanye. Uluhlu lwakho lugcinwa ngokuzenzekelayo kwezi diski zifanayo apho umculo uvela khona - ngoko unokufikelela kulo naphi na nge-USB stick okanye i-hard drive yangaphandle, kungakhathaliseki ukuba loluphi ikhompyutha olusetyenziswe ukudala uluhlu. I-playlist yangoku inokugcinwa ngokuzenzekelayo xa uphuma, ize ibuyiselwe xa uqalisa kwakhona.

📱 I-smartphone yakho iba lulawulo olukude kunye nesixhobo sokukhupha i-streaming
Vula ulawulo olukude olwakhelwe ngaphakathi (ngaphandle kokufaka usetyenziso olongezelelweyo) kuso nasiphi na isixhobo esiphathwayo. Skena ikhowudi ye-QR uze ulawule i-InstantDj naphi na: qala uze umise ukudlala, tshintsha ingoma, lawula ivolumu, lungisa kwakhona i-playlist kwaye ufake iingoma ezintsha ngokusebenzisa isikhangeli seengoma seselfowuni. Ukuba uyafuna, ungasasaza nesandi esidlalayo ngqo kwi-smartphone xa kufuneka ushiye indawo yomsitho, kodwa itheko liqhubeke.

⌚ Ukubalwa kwangaphambili okuchanekileyo kwexesha lokuqala lokudlala
Ixesha lokuqala lokudlala lazo zonke iingoma ezisaseleyo kwi-playlist libalwa ngokuzenzekelayo lize liboniswe. Ngale ndlela uyazi nanini na ukuba ingoma iza kuqala nini kwaye unokucwangcisa kakuhle ukuhamba kwexesha lomsitho. Ukubalwa kwexesha kuchaneke ukuya kumzuzwana kwaye kuthathela ingqalelo amaxesha okuvela nokuphela kwesandi, amaxesha okuthula, uhlengahlengiso lwesantya, njl.

🔌 Izinzile kuwo wonke umnyhadala
Isiphumo esiphambili kunye nesiphumo see-headphone zinokumiselwa ngokwahlukeneyo kwizixhobo ezithile, kunikwe amagama ezixhobo zakho kwaye ziphinde zisebenze ngokuzenzekelayo xa isixhobo sifumaneka. Ukuba isixhobo sesandi siqhawulwa ngempazamo, i-InstantDj iya kuqhubeka nokusikhangela ngasemva ize iqhubeke nokudlala nje ukuba sibuyile - okanye itshintshele kwesinye isixhobo osikhethileyo. Ukongeza, inkqubo ilawula izixhobo zesandi ngeenkqubo zenkqubo ezizimeleyo, ukuze ukukhutshwa kwesandi kuchane, kungaphazamiseki naphantsi komthwalo omkhulu wenkqubo - ukuze kungabikho nto ithi cwaka xa ibalulekile.

⌨️ Khawuleza ngekhibhodi
Izenzo ezisetyenziswa rhoqo zinokwabelwa kwizishunqulelo zekhibhodi onokuzicwangcisa ngokukhululekileyo - ukusuka ekumameleni kwangaphambili nasekutsibeni ekudlalweni kwee-headphone ukuya ekufakeni iingoma ezikhethiweyo kwi-playlist. Ngaloo ndlela umsebenzi we-DJ uhamba kakuhle, nokuba usebenzisa ikhiphedi enamaqhosha anokucwangciswa.

💡 Licocekile kwaye lilungele ukuqalisa ngoko nangoko
Umphezulu wale mihla omnyama ucwangciswe ngokucacileyo, kulula ukuwuqonda kwaye uyilelwe ukusebenza ngokutyibilikayo. Imowudi yesikrini esigcweleyo ibeka umculo embindini wengqwalasela - ngaphandle kweziphazamiso.
