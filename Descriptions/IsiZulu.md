I-InstantDj yathuthukiswa um-DJ ozinikele womdanso we-Ballroom, umdansi kanye nonjiniyela wezinhlelo ngenhloso yokwakha uhlelo lokusebenza lwe-DJ olusebenziseka kalula - ngaphandle kobunkimbinkimbi nezindleko zesoftware yobungcweti ye-DJ, kodwa olunemisebenzi eminingi kakhulu kunesidlali somculo noma sokusakaza esilula.
Ngaphakathi kohlelo lokusebenza kusebenza ukucutshungulwa komsindo kobungcweti okunokulawulwa kwethoni, ukulungiswa kwejubane, i-compressor, i-limiter, ukutholwa kokuthula ekuqaleni nasekupheleni, kanye nemiphumela eminingi - ukuze kutholakale umsindo onamandla nolinganiselayo ozwakala kahle ngokufanayo kusukela engomeni eyodwa kuya kwenye.
Lungisa wena ingoma elandelayo ngokuthula usebenzisa ama-headphone, ngesikhathi izethameli sezilalele ingoma edlalayo, noma uvumele ubusuku bonke buqhubeke ngokuzenzakalelayo ngokuphelele ngezinguquko ezibushelelezi kodwa ezicacile phakathi kwezingoma.

- Zonke izinhlobo zemicimbi yomdanso we-Ballroom enezingoma ezihlukaniswe ngokucacile

- Imishado, izinsuku zokuzalwa, imikhosi yezinhlangano, njll.
- Izifundo zomdanso, ama-workshop kanye nobusuku bokuzilolonga lapho ukukhethwa kwengoma, ijubane, ivolumu, njll. kufanele kulungiswe ngokuguquguqukayo
- Abafundisi abafuna ukulawula ukwehlisa nokuqala komculo kanye nevolumu bekude besendaweni yokudansa
🡆 Lolu hlelo lokusebenza lwenzelwe izethameli ezifuna ukudansa noma ukulalela umculo usondele kokwangempela ngangokunokwenzeka, futhi ingoma ngayinye ihlale ihlukaniswe ngokucacile. Ngakho-ke lolu hlelo lokusebenza alufaki ngamabomu i-live remixing ene-beatmatching.

Okugqamile

🎧 Ukulalela kusengaphambili ngesikhathi esisodwa njengom-DJ wangempela
I-InstantDj isekela okukhipha umsindo okubili okuhluke ngokuphelele, noma amadivayisi amabili ahlukene. Umculo wezethameli udlula kokukhipha okuyinhloko, kanti ngokukhipha kwama-headphone ulalela ingoma elandelayo kusengaphambili ngesikhathi esifanayo ngaphandle kokuba abanye bayizwe, uthole indawo efanele yokuqala futhi ulungiselele ushintsho. Yilokhu kanye okwenza umehluko phakathi kwesidlali esilula nokuba u-DJ wangempela.

🎚️ Okuzenzakalelayo ngokuphelele noma ngesandla
Ngokudlala okuzenzakalelayo, i-playlist yakho idlala ngokwayo futhi ingoma ngayinye idlulela ngokushelela kwelandelayo - okulungele imikhosi lapho ufuna ukunaka ezinye izinto. Noma nini lapho ufuna khona, ungaphinde uzilawule ngokwakho: izikhathi zokufaka nokukhipha umsindo kancane, ukugqagqana kwezingoma kanye nokuqala kwangaphambili kwengoma elandelayo kungasethwa ngokukhululeka, futhi kungagcinwa ngisho nangengoma ngayinye ngokwehlukana.

🎛️ Umsindo wobungcweti onokujula
Ngemuva kwesixhumi esibonakalayo esicacile kunokucutshungulwa komsindo okunamandla: ukulinganisa kwethoni kwamabhendi amathathu okuvamile (i-bass, ama-mid kanye nama-high), ukulawulwa okuhlukene kwejubane nethoni, i-echo, umsindo wendawo, i-compressor, i-limiter/maximizer, i-Harmonic Exciter, ukunwetshwa kwe-stereo, i-transient shaper enama-preset amaningi kanye ne-chorus, flanger ne-phaser. I-normalizer eguquguqukayo ilinganisa ngokuzenzakalelayo iziqephu zengoma ezirekhodwe ngamavolumu ahlukene, ukuze kungabi nalutho olugqama kakhulu noma olulahleka. Wonke amasethingi angagcinwa unomphela engomeni ngayinye.

✀ Ukuhlukaniswa kwamatrack omsindo nge-AI kuze kufike kumatrack angu-6
Ngemva kokulandwa kwemodeli ye-AI efanele, kungahlukaniswa ngokuzenzakalelayo kuze kufike kumatrack omsindo angu-6: amazwi, izigubhu, i-bass nezinye izinsimbi. Ngaleyo ndlela ungagqamisa noma uthulise ngamunye wamatrack ngokuguquguqukayo. Ku-Windows nase-Linux, ukuhlukaniswa kwamatrack omsindo nge-AI kusekelwa yi-NVIDIA CUDA, okwenza ukucutshungulwa kusheshe cishe izikhathi eziyishumi kunokusebenzisa i-CPU.

🥁 I-BPM ihlale ibonakala
Ukutholwa okuzenzakalelayo kwe-BPM kunquma ijubane lezingoma zakho - ngendlela emile kuyo yonke ingoma nangendlela eguquguqukayo ngesikhathi sangempela eduze nendawo yamanje yokudlala. Ezingomeni ezinohlobo noma uhlobo lomdanso, uma kunezindawo ze-BPM ezilondoloziwe, kuboniswa izindawo zejubane ezinemibala kanye nokuhlukaniswa okuqondakala kalula kusukela kokuthi "kancane kakhulu" kuye kokuthi "ngokushesha kakhulu". Ngaleyo ndlela ungakwazi ukulungisa ijubane lokudlala lihambisane nohlobo lomdanso wengoma kanye nezethameli zakho zomdanso.

📂 Umculo wakho uhlelwe ngendlela ephelele
I-song browser isesha amadrayivu namafolda akho ngokushesha okukhulu - ungakhetha imodi ye-Explorer noma amafolda akho owakhetha ngokukhululeka, futhi uma uthanda ibheke nangamafolda angaphansi. Hlela ngegama, idethi yokuguqulwa, isikhathi sokugcina sokudlalwa, i-BPM, ubude noma isilinganiso. Amafayela amasha avela ngokuzenzakalelayo ngenxa yokuqashwa kwamafolda, futhi ngokuhudula nokudedela ungafaka izingoma ngqo eqoqweni lakho noma ku-playlist. Ngezilinganiso zezinkanyezi kanye nezinhlobo nezigaba ezinemibala, ungakwazi ukulawula kalula namalabhulali amakhulu. Izici ezinemininingwane zefayela lomsindo zibonisa ifomethi, i-bitrate, izinga, i-dynamics, impendulo yefrikhwensi kanye nomlando ophelele wokudlala.

💾 Amaplaylist ahamba nawe
Londoloza noma yimaphi amaplaylist amaningi anamagama futhi uwalayishe ngokuchofoza okukodwa. Uhlu lwakho lugcinwa ngokuzenzakalelayo kumadivayisi afanayo okugcina lapho umculo uvela khona - ngakho unokufinyelela kuwo yonke indawo nge-USB stick noma i-hard drive yangaphandle, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi uhlu lwakhiwe kukhompyutha yiphi. I-playlist yamanje ingagcinwa ngokuzenzakalelayo lapho uphuma, uma uthanda, bese ibuyiselwa ekuqaleni okulandelayo.

📱 I-smartphone yakho iba yisilawuli kude kanye nedivayisi yokukhipha ukusakazwa komsindo
Yenza kusebenze isilawuli kude esididiyelwe (ngaphandle kokufaka olunye uhlelo lokusebenza) kunoma iyiphi idivayisi yeselula. Skena ikhodi ye-QR bese ulawula i-InstantDj noma yikuphi: qala futhi umise ukudlala, shintsha ingoma, lawula ivolumu, hlela kabusha i-playlist futhi ufake izingoma ezintsha emgqeni usebenzisa i-song explorer yeselula. Uma uthanda, ungasakaza umsindo obukhoma ngqo ku-smartphone yakho uma kufanele ushiye indawo yomcimbi kodwa ufuna iphathi iqhubeke.

⌚ Ukubalwa okunembile kusengaphambili kwesikhathi sokuqala sokudlalwa
Isikhathi sokuqala sokudlalwa kwazo zonke izingoma ezisasele ku-playlist sibalwa futhi siboniswe ngokuzenzakalelayo. Ngaleyo ndlela uyazi nganoma yisiphi isikhathi ukuthi ingoma izoqala nini futhi ungahlela kahle ukuhamba kwesikhathi komcimbi. Ukubalwa kwesikhathi kunemba ngesekhondi futhi kubheka izikhathi zokufaka nokukhipha umsindo kancane, izikhathi zokungenzi lutho, ukulungiswa kwejubane, njll.

🔌 Izinzile kuwo wonke umcimbi
Okukhipha okuyinhloko nokwama-headphone kungasethwa ngokwehlukana kumadivayisi amisiwe, kunganikezwa amagama akho futhi kusebenze ngokuzenzakalelayo futhi lapho idivayisi isitholakala. Uma idivayisi yomsindo inqanyulwa ngephutha, i-InstantDj iyaqhubeka nokuyifuna ngemuva futhi iqhubeke nokudlala lapho isibuya - noma ishintshele kwenye idivayisi oyikhethile. Ngaphezu kwalokho, uhlelo lulawula amadivayisi omsindo ngezinqubo zesistimu ezihlukanisiwe, ukuze okukhipha umsindo kuhlale kunembile futhi kungaphazamiseki ngisho nalapho uhlelo luthwele kakhulu - ukuze kungathuli lutho ngesikhathi esibalulekile.

⌨️ Shesha ngekhibhodi
Izenzo ezivamile zingabelwa izinqamuleli zekhibhodi ozikhethela zona - kusukela ekulaleleni kusengaphambili nasekweqeni ngaphakathi kokudlala kwama-headphone kuye ekufakeni izingoma ezikhethiwe ku-playlist. Ngaleyo ndlela ukuba u-DJ kuhamba kahle, ngisho noma usebenzisa ikhiphedi engahlelwa.

💡 Kuhleleke kahle futhi kulungele ukuqala ngokushesha
Isixhumi esibonakalayo sanamuhla esimnyama sihleleke ngokucacile, siqondakala kalula futhi senzelwe ukusebenza ngokushelela. Imodi yesikrini esigcwele ibeka umculo phakathi nendawo - ngaphandle kweziphazamiso.
