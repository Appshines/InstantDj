I-InstantDj yathuthukiswa ngu-DJ ozinikele we-Ballroom, umdansi kanye nonjiniyela wesoftware, ngenhloso yokwakha uhlelo lwe-DJ olusebenziseka kalula - ngaphandle kobunzima nezindleko zesofthiwe ye-DJ yobungcweti, kodwa lunemisebenzi eminingi kakhulu kunesidlali somculo noma se-stream esilula.
Ngaphakathi kohlelo kusebenza ukucutshungulwa komsindo kobungcweti okunokulinganisa komsindo, ukulungiswa kwejubane, i-compressor, i-limiter, ukutholwa kokuthula ekuqaleni nasekupheleni, kanye nemiphumela eminingi - ukuze kutholakale umsindo ogcwele, olinganiselayo futhi ozwakala kahle ngokufanayo kusukela engomeni iye kwenye.
Lungisa wena ingoma elandelayo ngokuthula usebenzisa ama-headphone, ngenkathi izethameli zisalalela ingoma edlalayo, noma uvumele ubusuku bonke buhambe ngokuzenzakalela ngezinguquko ezithambile kodwa ezicacile.

Ilungele:
- Ukulalela umculo ekhaya ukuze uzijabulise, ngekhwalithi yomsindo enhle kakhulu kanye nokulawula ngeselula usebenzisa i-smartphone
- Imikhosi yangasese yokudlala umculo ngesilawuli-kude, ngaphandle kokuthi uhlale phambi kwekhompyutha
- Zonke izinhlobo zemicimbi yomdanso we-Ballroom enezingoma ezihlukaniswe ngokucacile
- Amaphathi omdanso amancane naphakathi kanye nemicimbi ye-Social Dance lapho kushintshanwa khona abalingani
- Imishado, izinsuku zokuzalwa, imikhosi yamakilabhu, njll.
- Amakilasi omdanso, ama-workshop kanye nobusuku bokuziqeqesha lapho ukukhethwa kwengoma, ijubane, ivolumu, njll. kufanele kulungiswe kalula
- Abaqeqeshi abafuna ukuqala ukudlala nokulawula ivolumu bekude besenkundleni yomdanso
🡆 Lolu hlelo lwenzelwe izethameli ezifuna ukudansa noma ukulalela umculo ngendlela esondele kakhulu kweyokuqala, futhi ingoma ngayinye ihlale ihlukaniswe ngokucacile. Ngakho-ke, lolu hlelo alufaki ngamabomu i-live remixing ene-beatmatching.

Amaphuzu avelele

🎧 Lalela kuqala ngokufana ne-DJ wangempela
I-InstantDj isekela imiphumela emibili yomsindo ehlukaniswe ngokuphelele noma amadivayisi amabili ahlukene. Umphumela oyinhloko udlalela izethameli umculo, kanti ngomphumela wama-headphone ulalela ingoma elandelayo ngesikhathi esifanayo, ingazwakali kwabanye, uthole indawo efanele yokuqala futhi ulungiselele ushintsho. Yilo kanye lolu hlelo lokusebenza olwenza umehluko phakathi kwesidlali esilula nokuba yi-DJ wangempela.

🎚️ Okuzenzakalelayo ngokuphelele noma ngesandla
Ngokudlala okuzenzakalelayo, i-playlist yakho iqhubeka yodwa futhi ingoma ngayinye idlulela ngokushelela kwelandelayo - kuhle emikhosini lapho ufuna ukunaka ezinye izinto. Noma nini lapho ufuna, ungaphinde ulawule wena: izikhathi zokufaka nokukhipha umsindo kancane, ukugqagqana kwezingoma kanye nokuqala ngaphambi kwesikhathi kwengoma elandelayo kungasethwa ngokukhululekile, futhi kungagcinwa ngisho ngokwehlukana engomeni ngayinye.

🎛️ Umsindo wobungcweti onobubanzi
Ngemuva kobuso obucacile kufihlwe ukucutshungulwa komsindo okunamandla: ukulinganisa komsindo kwendabuko kwamabhendi amathathu (ama-bass, ama-mids nama-treble), ukulawulwa okuhlukene kwejubane nokuphakama kwethoni, i-compressor, i-limiter/maximizer, i-harmonic exciter, ukunwetshwa kwe-stereo, i-transient shaper, i-echo enama-preset amaningi kanye ne-chorus, i-flanger ne-phaser. I-normalizer eguquguqukayo ilinganisa ngokuzenzakalelayo izigaba zengoma ezirekhodwe ngamavolumu ahlukene, ukuze kungabikho lutho olugqama kakhulu noma olulahleka. Zonke izilungiselelo zingagcinwa unomphela engomeni ngayinye.

🥁 Hlala uyibona i-BPM
Ukutholwa okuzenzakalelayo kwe-BPM kunquma ijubane lezingoma zakho - ngokungaguquki engomeni yonke nangokuguquguquka ngesikhathi sangempela eduze kwendawo edlalwayo njengamanje. Ezingomeni ezinohlobo noma uhlobo lomdanso, izindawo zejubane ezinemibala kanye nokuhlukaniswa okuqondakala kalula kusukela kokuthi "kancane kakhulu" kuya kokuthi "ngokushesha kakhulu" kuyatholakala uma kunezindawo ze-BPM ezibekiwe. Ngakho ungakwazi ukulungisa ijubane lokudlala ukuze lihambisane nohlobo lomdanso wengoma kanye nezethameli zakho zomdanso.

📂 Umculo wakho uhlelekile ngokuphelele
I-song browser isesha amadrayivu namafolda akho ngokushesha okukhulu - ngokukhetha imodi ye-Explorer noma amafolda akho owakhethile ngokukhululekile, futhi uma uthanda ifake namafolda angaphansi ngokuphindaphindiwe. Hlela ngegama, usuku lokushintshwa, isikhathi sokugcina sokudlalwa, i-BPM, ubude noma isilinganiso. Amafayela amasha avela ngokuzenzakalelayo ngenxa yokuqashwa kwamafolda, futhi ngokuhudula nokudedela ufaka izingoma ngqo eqoqweni lakho noma ku-playlist. Ngezilinganiso zezinkanyezi kanye nezinhlobo nezigaba ezinemibala, ungakwazi ukulawula kalula ngisho namalabhulali amakhulu. Izici ezinemininingwane zefayela lomsindo zibonisa ifomethi, i-bitrate, ileveli, i-dynamics, impendulo yefrikhwensi kanye nomlando wokudlala ophelele.

💾 Ama-playlist ahamba nawe
Gcina noma iyiphi inombolo yama-playlist anamagama futhi uwalayishe ngokuchofoza kanye. Ama-list akho agcinwa ngokuzenzakalelayo kumadivayisi afanayo okugcina lapho umculo utholakala khona - ngakho ungawathola noma kuphi usebenzisa i-USB stick noma i-hard drive yangaphandle, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi lolu hlu lwakhiwe kuyiphi ikhompyutha. I-playlist yamanje ingagcinwa ngokuzenzakalelayo lapho uvala uhlelo futhi ibuyiselwe ekuqaleni okulandelayo.

📱 I-smartphone yakho iba isilawuli-kude nedivayisi yokukhipha i-streaming
Vula isilawuli-kude esakhelwe ngaphakathi (ngaphandle kokufaka olunye uhlelo lokusebenza) kunoma iyiphi idivayisi yeselula. Skena ikhodi ye-QR bese ulawula i-InstantDj noma kuphi: qala futhi umise ukudlala, shintsha izingoma, lawula ivolumu, hlela kabusha i-playlist futhi ufake izingoma ezintsha usebenzisa i-song explorer yeselula. Uma uthanda, ungadlala ngisho nomsindo obukhoma ngqo ku-smartphone uma kufanele ushiye indawo yomcimbi kodwa ufuna iphathi iqhubeke.

⌚ Ukubalwa kusenesikhathi okunembile kwesikhathi sokuqala sokudlala
Isikhathi sokuqala sokudlala sazo zonke izingoma ezisazolandela ku-playlist sibalwa futhi siboniswe ngokuzenzakalelayo. Ngakho uyazi ngaso sonke isikhathi ukuthi ingoma izoqala nini futhi ungahlela kahle ukuhamba kwesikhathi komcimbi. Ukubalwa kwesikhathi kunembile kuze kube yisekhondi futhi kubheka izikhathi zokufaka nokukhipha umsindo kancane ezibekiwe, izikhathi zokungasebenzi, ukulungiswa kwejubane, njll.

🔌 Izinzile kuwo wonke umcimbi
Imiphumela eyinhloko neyama-headphone ingasethwa ngokwehlukana kumadivayisi athile, inikwe amagama akho futhi iphinde isebenze ngokuzenzakalelayo lapho idivayisi isitholakala. Uma idivayisi yomsindo inqanyulwa ngephutha, i-InstantDj iyaqhubeka nokuyifuna ngemuva futhi iqhubeke nokudlala lapho ibuyile - noma ishintshele kwenye idivayisi oyikhethile. Ngaphezu kwalokho, uhlelo lulawula amadivayisi omsindo ngezinqubo zesistimu ezihlukanisiwe, ukuze okukhipha umsindo kube okunembile futhi kuqhubeke ngaphandle kokuphazamiseka ngisho noma uhlelo lusetshenziswa kakhulu - ukuze kungathuli lutho lapho kubalulekile.

⌨️ Ngokushesha ngekhibhodi
Izenzo ezivamile zingabelwa izinqamuleli zekhibhodi ozikhethayo - kusukela ekulaleleni kuqala nasekugxumeni ekudlaleni kwama-headphone kuze kufake izingoma ezikhethiwe ku-playlist. Ngaleyo ndlela umsebenzi we-DJ uqhubeka kahle, ngisho noma usebenzisa ikhiphedi ehlelekayo.

💡 Kuhleleke futhi kulungele ukuqala ngokushesha
Ubuso besimanje obumnyama buhlelekile ngokucacile, buqondakala kalula futhi benzelwe ukusebenza okushelelayo. Imodi yesikrini esigcwele ibeka umculo phakathi nendawo - ngaphandle kweziphazamiso.

🔒 Kusebenza ngokuphelele endaweni
I-InstantDj isebenza kuphela ngamafayela akho ngqo kudivayisi yakho. Akukho ukulayisha, akukho ukungena ngemvume, akukho ukulandelela, akukho akhawunti - futhi akukho uxhumano lwe-inthanethi oludingekayo. Qaphela: Ukuze ufinyelele usebenzisa iselula, ngokwemvelo udinga inethiwekhi yendawo kanye/noma ukufinyelela ku-inthanethi, kuye ngokuthi ukulungise kanjani ukufinyelela kweselula.

🔇 Akukona ukuba yi-DJ yobungcweti
Lolu hlelo aluhloselwe ukuxuba komculo kwe-DJ yobungcweti okunama-deck amaningi, ama-sampler, ama-loop, ama-MIDI controller, amadivayisi angaphandle, i-live remixing, i-scratch, i-beatmatching, i-key matching, njll.
