# InstantDj

InstantDj is a DJ application for parties, events and mobile DJs. Preview, play, and control your music on the go like a DJ — professional sound and precise transitions. InstantDj combines main playback, headphone preview, playlist management and Song Browser in one shared workspace.

## About InstantDj

InstantDj was developed by a dedicated ballroom DJ, dancer, and software developer with the goal of creating a user-friendly DJ app — without the complexity and cost of professional DJ software, but with significantly more features than a simple music or streaming player.

At the heart of the app is professional audio processing with equalization, tempo adjustment, compressor, limiter, intro/outro silence detection, and a wide range of effects — for a powerful, balanced sound that sounds equally good from one song to the next.

Adjust the next track at your leisure over the headphones while the audience is already listening to the current song, or let entire evenings run fully automatically with smooth yet clearly defined transitions.

**Ideal for:**

- Personal music enjoyment at home with excellent sound quality and mobile control via smartphone
- Private parties with remote-controlled music playback, without having to sit at the computer yourself
- All kinds of ballroom dance events with clearly separated songs
- Small and medium-sized dance parties and social dance events with partner changes
- Weddings, birthdays, club celebrations, etc.
- Dance classes, workshops, and practice evenings where the song selection, tempo, volume, etc. need to be adjusted flexibly
- Instructors who want to remotely control playback and volume from the dance floor

> 🡆 This app is designed for an audience that wants to dance or listen to music as close to the original as possible, with each track remaining clearly separated. For this reason, live remixing with beatmatching has deliberately been omitted from this app.

### Overview of key features

- 🎛️ Extensive sound and effect processing — can be saved individually for each song
- 🎧 Two separate audio outputs: the main output for the audience, and the headphone output for cueing
- 🎚️ Fully automatic playback with intro/outro silence detection and therefore precise crossfades (Auto-playback)
- 🔊 Dynamic normalizer for consistently even volume and compressor for controlled sound
- 📈 Real-time waveform, spectrum, and BPM display
- 📂 Integrated song browser with search, sorting by BPM, rating, recently played, etc.
- 📱 Smartphone remote control with switchable live audio stream (main output or cueing)
- ⭐ Ratings as well as genres/dances and categories for organizing your music collection
- 💾 Save, load, and automatically back up playlists with specific settings for each song
- ✨ Intelligent background analysis of duration, BPM, level, dynamics, frequency distribution, and more
- 🎵 Support for all common and high-quality audio formats
- ⌚ Precise advance calculation of the start time of all upcoming tracks

### Highlights

**🎧 Cue in parallel like a real DJ**
InstantDj supports two completely separate audio outputs or devices. The music for your audience plays through the main output, while you use the headphone output to preview the next track in parallel and inaudibly to everyone else, find the right entry point, and prepare the transition. This workflow is precisely what distinguishes a simple player from genuine DJing.

**🎚️ Fully automatic or manual**
With Auto-playback, your playlist plays through independently and smoothly fades each track into the next — ideal for parties where you want to take care of other things. Whenever you want, you can take over again: fade-in and fade-out times, track overlap, and the early start of the next song can be freely configured and even saved individually for each song.

**🎛️ Pro-level sound with depth**
Behind the clear interface is powerful audio processing: classic three-band equalization (bass, mids, treble), separate tempo and pitch control, compressor, limiter/maximizer, harmonic exciter, stereo widening, transient shaper, echo with numerous presets, as well as chorus, flanger, and phaser. The dynamic normalizer automatically balances differently leveled song passages so that nothing stands out or gets lost. All settings can be stored permanently for each song.

**🥁 BPM always in view**
Automatic BPM detection determines the tempo of your tracks — statically for the entire song and dynamically in real time around the current playback position. For tracks with a genre or type, configured BPM ranges provide colored speed zones and an easy-to-understand classification from "very slow" to "very fast". This lets you adjust the playback speed appropriately for the track's dance type and optimally for your dance audience.

**📂 Your music, perfectly organized**
The song browser searches your drives and folders at lightning speed — either in Explorer mode or through freely selectable custom folders, optionally recursively including subfolders. Sort by name, modification date, recently played, BPM, duration, or rating. New files appear automatically through folder monitoring, and you can drag and drop tracks directly into your collection or playlist. With star ratings as well as colored genres and categories, you can effortlessly keep even large libraries under control. Detailed audio file properties reveal the format, bitrate, level, dynamics, frequency response, and complete playback history.

**💾 Playlists that travel with you**
Save any number of named playlists and load them with one click. Your lists are automatically stored on the same drives where the music comes from — so you can access them anywhere via your USB stick or external hard drive, regardless of which computer the list was created on. If desired, the current playlist is automatically saved when you exit and restored the next time you start the app.

**📱 Your smartphone becomes a remote control and streaming output device**
Activate the integrated remote control (with no extra app installation) from any mobile device. Scan the QR code and control InstantDj from anywhere: start and stop playback, change tracks, adjust the volume, rearrange the playlist, and add new songs through the mobile song browser. If desired, you can even stream the live audio directly to your smartphone when you need to leave the venue while the party continues.

**⌚ Precise advance calculation of the start time**
The playback start time of every remaining track in the playlist is calculated and displayed automatically. This means you always know when a song will start and can plan the event schedule optimally. The time calculation is accurate to the second and takes the configured fade-in and fade-out times, idle times, tempo adjustments, etc. into account.

**🔌 Stable at every gig**
The main and headphone outputs can be assigned separately to fixed devices, given custom device names, and automatically reactivated as soon as a device becomes available. If an audio device is accidentally disconnected, InstantDj continues searching for it in the background and resumes playback as soon as it returns — or switches to another device of your choice. The program also controls audio devices through isolated system processes, ensuring exact audio output without interruptions even under heavy system load — so nothing goes silent when it matters.

**⌨️ Fast with the keyboard**
Frequent actions can be assigned to freely configurable keyboard shortcuts — from previewing and seeking through headphone playback to inserting selected tracks into the playlist. This makes DJing flow smoothly, even with a programmable keypad.

**💡 Tidy and ready to go**
The modern, dark interface is clearly structured, easy to understand, and designed for smooth operation. A full-screen mode puts the music front and center — without distractions.

**🔒 Completely local**
InstantDj works exclusively with your files directly on your device. No uploads, no sign-in, no tracking, no account — and no internet connection required. Note: Mobile access naturally requires a local network and/or internet access, depending on how you have configured mobile access.

**🔇 Not professional DJing**
This app is not intended for professional DJ mixing with multiple decks, samplers, loops, MIDI controllers, external devices, live remixing, scratching, beatmatching, key matching, etc.

## Linux downloads

Get the latest version from the **[Releases page](../../releases/latest)**. The following packages are provided:

| File | For |
|------|-----|
| `InstantDj-x.y.z-x86_64.AppImage` | Any Linux distribution — no installation required |
| `instantdj_x.y.z_amd64.deb` | Debian, Ubuntu, Linux Mint |
| `instantdj-x.y.z.x86_64.rpm` | Fedora, openSUSE |
| `InstantDj-x.y.z-linux-x64.tar.gz` | Generic archive for all other distributions |

### AppImage (recommended)

```bash
chmod +x InstantDj-*.AppImage
./InstantDj-*.AppImage
```

### Debian / Ubuntu / Mint

```bash
sudo apt install ./instantdj_*_amd64.deb
```

### Fedora / openSUSE

```bash
sudo dnf install ./instantdj-*.x86_64.rpm
```

### Generic archive

```bash
tar -xzf InstantDj-*-linux-x64.tar.gz
cd InstantDj
./InstantDj
```

### System requirements

- 64-bit Linux (x86_64)
- X11 or Wayland desktop session
- PulseAudio or PipeWire audio server
